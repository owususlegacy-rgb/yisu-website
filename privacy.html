Privacy

Privacy Policy

What we collect when you book with us, why, and how it's used. We only ask for what's needed to confirm your appointment.

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

When you book an appointment through this website, we ask for:

  • Your full name
  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your email address (optional)
  • Any notes you choose to add about your appointment

We don't collect payment card details ourselves — these are handled directly and securely by our payment processor, Yoco, and never pass through our systems.

Why we collect it

Your name and phone number are used to confirm your booking with the salon over WhatsApp, and to reach you if your appointment needs to be rescheduled. Your email, if provided, may be used for the same purpose. Notes you add are shared with your stylist so they can prepare for your visit.

How it's used and stored

Your booking details are sent, via WhatsApp, to the number used by Yisu to manage appointments. We do not currently store your information in a separate database — it exists as a WhatsApp message confirming your booking. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Yoco, a licensed South African payment provider. Yoco's own privacy policy governs how your payment information is handled. We recommend reviewing it if you have questions about payment data specifically.

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, request that it be corrected, or ask that it be deleted. To do so, contact us on WhatsApp using the number below.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or analytics software. If that changes, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our booking process evolves. Continued use of the booking system after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests relating to your personal information, can be sent to us on WhatsApp: 068 096 5235.
