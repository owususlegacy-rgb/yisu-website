Where it began

Built on an idea, not a formula

Every Yisu appointment starts with the same question the house was founded on: what actually suits you, not what's trending.

Mama Janet

Founder & CEO, Yisu

Mama Janet, a Ghanaian entrepreneur with a passion for beauty and community, founded Yisu Beauty in 2021 at Blueberry Square in Roodepoort. Her vision was to create a one-stop salon where clients could enjoy professional braiding, haircuts, nails, and lashes in a warm, welcoming environment.

From the start, Mama Janet focused on quality service, skilled staff, and a clean, modern space that reflected her Ghanaian heritage and commitment to excellence. As CEO, she leads by example — training her team, managing operations, and ensuring every client leaves feeling confident and cared for. Today, Yisu Beauty stands as a local success story, built on her dedication, cultural pride, and belief that great service creates loyal customers.
