Terms & Conditions

The short version: your booking fee secures your appointment, the salon settles the service cost, and we'll always try to make things right if something goes wrong on our end.

Last updated: 2026

Who this applies to

These terms apply to anyone booking an appointment with Yisu through this website (yisubeuaty.com), located at 1489 Blueberry St, Honeydew, Johannesburg, 2040, South Africa. By completing a booking, you agree to the terms below.

Booking fees

Each service you select carries a R50 booking fee, paid online at the time of booking. This fee secures your appointment slot. It is separate from, and does not cover, the cost of the service itself — that amount is paid directly to Yisu at the salon, on the day of your appointment.

Payment

Booking fees are processed securely through Yoco. We do not see or store your card details — they are handled entirely by Yoco's payment platform. Once your payment succeeds, you'll be redirected back to this site and prompted to confirm your booking details over WhatsApp.

Cancellations and rescheduling

Booking fees are non-refundable, with one exception: if Yisu is unable to honour your confirmed appointment due to a scheduling error on our part (for example, overbooking), your R50 fee will be refunded in full.

If you need to cancel, or if we need to move your appointment for any other reason, we're happy to reschedule you to a new date and time at no additional cost. Your original booking fee carries over — you won't be charged again.

We ask that you let us know as early as possible if your plans change, using the WhatsApp number below.

Arriving for your appointment

Please arrive on time for your booked slot. If you're running late, message us on WhatsApp so we can let you know whether your stylist can still accommodate you. Repeated no-shows without notice may affect your ability to book online in future.

Accuracy of information

You're responsible for providing accurate contact details (name and phone number) when booking, so we can reach you to confirm or discuss your appointment. Yisu is not responsible for missed confirmations resulting from incorrect details provided at booking.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time as our booking process evolves. The version in effect at the time of your booking is the one that applies to that booking.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to us directly on WhatsApp: 068 096 5235.
